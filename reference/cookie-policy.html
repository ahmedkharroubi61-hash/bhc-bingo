Do you need cookie consent? Yes — because this site is designed to support optional analytics cookies. Under the GDPR/ePrivacy rules (for EU visitors) and good practice under Tunisian data-protection law, non-essential cookies may only be set after the user gives clear, prior consent. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent. This site is built to honour that: nothing beyond strictly necessary cookies loads until you choose “Accept all” or enable a category.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (such as local storage) work in a comparable way. They help the site function, remember your choices, and — where you allow it — measure how the site is used.

How we use cookies

We group cookies into three categories. You control the optional ones through our consent banner and the Cookie Settings link, available at any time.

  • Use the cookie banner shown on your first visit, or reopen it any time via Cookie Settings.
