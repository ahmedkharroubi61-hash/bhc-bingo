4. Legal basis for processing

Depending on the applicable law, we rely on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract — to fulfil your orders.
  • Consent — for marketing emails and non-essential (analytics/marketing) cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — for invoicing, accounting and consumer-law record-keeping.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the site and prevent fraud, balanced against your rights.

Processing of personal data in Tunisia is governed by Loi organique n° 2004-63 du 27 juillet 2004 relating to the protection of personal data, overseen by the INPDP (Instance Nationale de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel). If you are located in the European Union, we also aim to align with the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).
